The big picture

Two families of planets

The four inner planets are small and rocky. Beyond the asteroid belt, the four outer planets are giant worlds with very different compositions.

INNER SOLAR SYSTEM · 1–4

Terrestrial planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars have solid surfaces and are relatively small. They formed in the hotter region close to the young Sun.

OUTER SOLAR SYSTEM · 5–8

Giant planets

Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants; Uranus and Neptune are ice giants. All have rings, and all have extensive moon systems.

Keep looking outward

Planets are only the beginning.

Dwarf planets, asteroids, comets and icy objects also orbit the Sun. Pluto, for example, is a fascinating dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt.
