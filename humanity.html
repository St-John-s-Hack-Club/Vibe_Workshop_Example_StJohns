Chapter 02 · The explorers

Humanity is
reaching outward.

We explore space to understand where we came from, how worlds work, whether life could exist elsewhere and how to protect our own planet.

How we explore

Many tools. One universe.

Not every mission needs an astronaut. Most of what we know about the Solar System comes from instruments and robotic explorers.

01 / HUMAN SPACEFLIGHT

People living and working in orbit

The International Space Station is a laboratory where crews study biology, materials, physics and how humans adapt to living in microgravity.

02 / ROBOTIC EXPLORATION

Rovers, landers and orbiters

Robots can travel to places that are difficult or dangerous for people. They photograph landscapes, analyse rocks and measure planetary environments.

03 / SPACE TELESCOPES

Observatories beyond the atmosphere

Telescopes such as Hubble and the James Webb Space Telescope observe planets, stars and galaxies using different wavelengths of light.

04 / EARTH OBSERVATION

Studying our own planet

Satellites monitor weather, oceans, forests, ice and atmospheric conditions. Space technology helps us understand changes on Earth.

A programme to watch

The return to the Moon

NASA's Artemis programme is developing the systems and partnerships needed for astronauts to return to the lunar surface and prepare for longer journeys.

NASA · ARTEMIS PROGRAMME

From lunar flyby to lunar landing

Artemis II flew astronauts around the Moon in April 2026. NASA's current plan places Artemis III in 2027 for tests in Earth orbit, with Artemis IV targeted for the first crewed lunar landing of the programme in 2028. Schedules can change as engineering and safety work progresses.

Preparing for the next lunar landing

Artemis III is planned as an in-orbit demonstration, followed by a targeted lunar landing on Artemis IV.

Europe is exploring, too

ESA's JUICE mission

Launched in 2023, the European Space Agency's JUICE spacecraft is travelling to Jupiter. It will study the giant planet and icy moons including Ganymede, Callisto and Europa. Arrival is expected in 2031.
